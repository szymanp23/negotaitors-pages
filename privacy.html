Privacy notice

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This notice explains how personal data is handled when you visit negotaitors.com or contact us. It covers our information website and correspondence, not data processing within a negotiation training application.

Who is responsible

Izabela Kot and Piotr Szymański, based in Switzerland, jointly operate NegotAItors.com and are responsible for the personal data we collect through this website and our correspondence.

For privacy questions or requests, contact: negotaitors.com@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

When you contact us through our form, we receive your name, email address, organisation if provided, and message. When you email us directly, we receive your email address and the information included in your correspondence.

We use this information to respond to enquiries, discuss our product and potential collaborations, and manage ongoing correspondence. Please provide only information relevant to your enquiry.

Our website hosting and form providers also process technical information, such as IP addresses and request information, to deliver their services and maintain security.

Services we use

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub processes technical data associated with visits. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.
  • Formspree receives and processes contact form submissions and forwards them to our email inbox. Submissions can also be stored in our Formspree account. See Formspree’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gmail is used to receive, store and reply to correspondence. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

These services involve processing outside Switzerland, including in the United States. Formspree states that its hosting infrastructure is located in the United States. Information about the providers’ international processing and transfer arrangements is available in their linked privacy documentation.

How long we keep correspondence

For enquiries that do not lead to a collaboration, we delete the correspondence from our Gmail mailbox and Formspree submission archive within six months after the correspondence has ended.

If an enquiry leads to a collaboration, we retain relevant correspondence for as long as needed to manage that relationship. Specific records may be kept longer where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

This six-month period applies to the correspondence we manage. Service providers’ technical logs and residual backups follow their own applicable retention and deletion procedures.

Analytics and external links

We do not use website analytics or advertising tracking tools.

Our website contains links to LinkedIn profiles. If you follow those links, LinkedIn’s own privacy terms apply.

Your rights

Under applicable data protection law, you may request information about your personal data and ask for inaccurate information to be corrected or data to be deleted. Additional rights, including data portability, may apply where the legal conditions are met.

Contact negotaitors.com@gmail.com to make a request. You may also contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

Updates

We will update this notice when our services or data handling practices change.
