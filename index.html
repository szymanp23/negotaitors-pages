The idea

The AI plays the character. The rules decide what happens.

Most AI role-play is a black box: you never know why the character changed its mind. Here the model never decides the outcome. It only speaks.

The AI

Speaks and observes

  • Plays the character, in character
  • Detects which behaviours the trainee showed
  • Writes the final debrief
The rules

Decide, in the open

  • Move the dials: mood, trust, willingness
  • Trigger events and injects
  • Determine success or failure

Scenarios

Four situations to practise

Every scenario is built around one character with their own goals, fears and red lines. Trainers can adapt all of it.

Access

The Closed Bridge

Floodwater has cut the district off. The only crossing is held by a militia commander who answers to a local council and wants guarantees. Get the relief trucks across.

Protection

Night at the Border Post

A group of displaced families is stranded at a closed crossing. The duty officer fears for their career. Win a medical transfer before dawn.

Essential services

The Last Water Tower

A besieging commander controls the pumping station that supplies a town. Persuade them to let a repair crew work safely.

Detention

The Detainee List

A regional security chief holds the only list of detainees. Build enough trust to get access, and a way for families to send news.

All characters and places are fictional.
